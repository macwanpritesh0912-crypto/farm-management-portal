Daily Planning SOP

  1. Morning meeting at fixed time.
  2. List all pending work.
  3. Set Top 3 priorities for the day.
  4. Assign workers.
  5. Review progress in evening.

Customer Order SOP

  1. Confirm customer order.
  2. Record name, city and quantity.
  3. Harvest required quantity.
  4. Pack produce carefully.
  5. Dispatch and update status.

Harvest SOP

  1. Harvest during cool hours.
  2. Separate damaged produce.
  3. Record quantity harvested.
  4. Pack according to order.
  5. Update harvest log.

Spraying SOP

  1. Check weather conditions.
  2. Prepare required solution.
  3. Wear safety equipment.
  4. Complete spray as scheduled.
  5. Clean equipment after use.

Fish Feeding SOP

  1. Observe fish behaviour.
  2. Feed according to schedule.
  3. Record feed quantity.
  4. Check feeding response.
  5. Report abnormalities.

Irrigation SOP

  1. Check soil moisture first.
  2. Inspect pumps and pipelines.
  3. Start irrigation as planned.
  4. Monitor water distribution.
  5. Record irrigation details.
