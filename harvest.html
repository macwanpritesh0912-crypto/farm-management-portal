Harvest SOP

  1. Harvest during cool morning hours.
  2. Use clean baskets and containers.
  3. Separate damaged produce.
  4. Record quantity immediately.
  5. Pack parcel orders first.
  6. Update harvest records daily.

Priority Rule

Customer orders have first priority. Harvest only according to confirmed orders whenever possible.
